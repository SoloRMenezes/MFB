ABOUT MFB

Connecting transport
with your operation.

MFB – Transporte e Logística, Lda. is an Angolan company based in Luanda, working in road transport and business logistics.

Bulk fuel and freight transport form part of the operational context supplied by the company. Each enquiry is assessed against the cargo, route and available equipment.
